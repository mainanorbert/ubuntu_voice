# DRC Peace and Humanitarian Support Agent Evaluation

## Question 1

Explain the DRC’s transition from Belgian colonial rule to the First Congo War. Include the independence date, Mobutu’s rise to power, the country’s former name, and the main forces involved in overthrowing Mobutu.

**Ground Truth Answer**

The DRC gained independence from Belgium on **30 June 1960**. In **1965**, Joseph-Désiré Mobutu seized power and later renamed the country **Zaire**. The First Congo War began in **1996** after the 1994 Rwandan genocide and the arrival of refugees and armed groups in eastern Zaire. Rwandan and Ugandan forces supported an alliance led by **Laurent-Désiré Kabila**, which overthrew Mobutu in **1997** and restored the country’s name to the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

## Question 3

What does Article 16 of the DRC Constitution protect, and which three international legal instruments support the same right?

**Ground Truth Answer**

Article 16 protects the **right to life** and prohibits the arbitrary deprivation of life. This protection is also supported by **Article 3 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights**, **Article 6 of the ICCPR**, and **Article 4 of the African Charter on Human and Peoples’ Rights**.

## Question 5

How many people required humanitarian assistance in the DRC according to OCHA, and how many people did the 2026 response plan aim to reach?

**Ground Truth Answer**

According to OCHA, nearly **15 million people** required humanitarian assistance in the DRC. Because of severe funding limitations, the **2026 response plan aimed to reach approximately 7.3 million people**.
