# Sudan Peace Agent Evaluation

## Question 1

**Question**

Explain why the Sudan Civil War began on **15 April 2023**. In your answer, identify the **two armed groups involved**, **name their commanders**, and **describe three specific causes** that led to the conflict.

**Ground Truth Answer**

The Sudan Civil War began on **15 April 2023** after tensions escalated between the **Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF)**, led by **General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan**, and the **Rapid Support Forces (RSF)**, led by **General Mohamed Hamdan Dagalo (Hemedti)**. Three major causes of the conflict were the power struggle between the two military leaders following the 2021 military coup, disagreements over integrating the RSF into the national army, and competition for control of government institutions, state resources, and Sudan's political future.

## Question 2

**Question**

What has been the humanitarian impact of the Sudan Civil War? In your answer, state the number of people requiring humanitarian assistance, the number of people displaced, the current confirmed death toll, and three main ways children have been affected.

**Ground Truth Answer**

The Sudan Civil War has created one of the world's largest humanitarian crises, with approximately 34 million people requiring humanitarian assistance. Nearly 13 million people have been displaced from their homes, including more than 10 million internally displaced people and over 3 million refugees in neighboring countries. The confirmed death toll is at least 59,000 people, although the actual number is believed to be much higher. Children have been affected primarily through interrupted education, severe malnutrition, and displacement.

## Question 3

**Question**

Which organizations in Sudan are responsible for (1) coordinating humanitarian response and emergency relief and (2) providing emergency medical care, civilian protection, and humanitarian assistance? Include the name of each organization, its primary focus, and its emergency hotline and email address.

**Ground Truth Answer**

The United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (UN OCHA) coordinates humanitarian response and emergency relief. Its primary focus is coordinating humanitarian assistance during emergencies. Its emergency hotline is +999 100 100 001, and its email address is emergency@ocha-demo.org.

The International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC) provides emergency medical care, civilian protection, and humanitarian assistance. Its primary focus is protecting civilians and delivering humanitarian aid during conflict. Its emergency hotline is +999 100 100 006, and its email address is contact@icrc-demo.org.
