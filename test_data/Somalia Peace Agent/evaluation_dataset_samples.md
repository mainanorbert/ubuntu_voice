## Question

Explain Somalia’s transition from independence and the First Republic to federalism. Include the independence date, the 1969 coup, the 1991 government collapse, the 2004 Transitional Federal Government, the 2012 provisional constitution, and three Federal Member States.

## Ground Truth Answer

Somalia became independent on **1 July 1960** and initially operated as a parliamentary republic. Major General Mohamed Siad Barre seized power in a **1969 military coup**, but his government collapsed in **1991**, creating political instability and armed conflict. The **Transitional Federal Government** was established in **2004**, followed by a **2012 provisional constitution** and the creation of Federal Member States, including **Puntland, Jubaland, and Galmudug**.

## Question 3

Which articles of the Somali Constitution protect the rights to life, freedom from torture, and personal liberty?

**Ground Truth Answer**

**Article 13** protects the right to life, **Article 14** prohibits torture and inhuman treatment, and **Article 15** protects personal liberty and security.
